Flesh and Stone
Spell, Aura, AoE, Stance, Physical
Level: (1—20)
Reservation: 25% Mana
Cooldown Time: 2.00 sec
Cast Time: Instant
Requires Level (16—70), (26—98) Str, (18—68) Dex
Buffs you, and affects nearby enemies with an aura, differently depending on your stance. Both stances are more impactful against enemies closer to you. Using the skill again alternates between Blood Stance and Sand Stance.
Base Aura radius is 2.8 metres

While in Blood Stance, Aura makes Enemies take up to (10—20)% more Physical Damage from Hits
While in Sand Stance, Buff makes you take up to (10—19)% less Damage from Enemies in Aura
