Cull the Weak Support
Exceptional, Support
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 120%
Requires Level (72—76), (114—120) Dex
Supports any skill that hits enemies. If normal or magic enemies are left below a percentage of maximum life after being hit by these skills, they will be killed.
Kill Normal and Magic Rarity Enemies that have (30—34)% Life or
lower when Hit by Supported Skills

Additional Effects From Quality:
Recover (0—2)% of Life when Supported Skills Cull an Enemy
"Beidat is the lesser of three evils. The other two desire only
to consume us, but under him, there is a chance to survive...
a chance to serve... if you can prove yourself useful."
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
