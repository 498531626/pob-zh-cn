Orb of Storms
Lightning, Spell, AoE, Chaining, Orb
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (5—20) Mana
Cooldown Time: 0.50 sec
Cast Time: 0.40 sec
Critical Strike Chance: 5.00%
Effectiveness of Added Damage: (80—110)%
AoE Radius: 28
Requires Level (4—70), (16—155) Int
Creates a stationary electrical orb that strikes enemies in its area of effect with beams of lightning that can then split to hit more enemies. Modifiers to cast speed will increase how frequently it does this. Casting this skill again will replace the previous orb.
Deals (3—304) to (8—913) Lightning Damage
Strikes every (2.55—3.5) seconds, and when you use a Lightning Skill near the Orb
Strikes every 0.5 seconds while Channelling a Lightning Skill near the Orb
Base radius is 2.8 metres
Beams Split towards targets within a base radius of 3.5 metres
Orb disappears after (10—29) Strikes
Beam Splits towards 3 additional targets

Additional Effects From Quality:
Beam Splits towards +(0—2) additional targets
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
