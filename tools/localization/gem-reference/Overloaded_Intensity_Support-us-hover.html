Overloaded Intensity Support
Exceptional, Spell, Critical, Support
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (72—76), (114—120) Int
Supports spells skills that gain Intensity.
Gain a stack of Overloaded Intensity for 10 seconds after
losing 6 Intensity with Supported Skills
Supported Skills have +(1—1.4)% to Critical Strike Chance per Overloaded Intensity
Supported Skills have +(40—50)% to Critical Strike Multiplier per Overloaded Intensity
(Maximum of 3 Overloaded Intensity. Gaining Overloaded Intensity refreshes the duration of existing stacks of Overloaded Intensity)

Additional Effects From Quality:

"I didn't design it to overload... let's call it a happy little accident!"
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
