Summon Spirit of Utula
Level: (1—20)
Cooldown Time: (80.00—20.00) sec
Cast Time: 1.00 sec
Summons an immortal chieftain minion of the Kitava tribe to fight alongside you for a duration. This minion grants you a buff which makes a percentage of your leech recover instantly. Shares a Cooldown with other Spirit Chieftain Skills.
Base duration is 12 seconds
Buff makes (25—50)% of Leech Instant
(10—20)% increased Buff Effect per Allocated Kitava Tattoo
This Skill's Cooldown does not recover during its effect
Trigger this Skill when you take a Savage Hit from a Unique Enemy
Minions' Level is equal to yours, up to a maximum of 85
