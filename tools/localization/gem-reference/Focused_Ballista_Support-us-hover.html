Focused Ballista Support
Projectile, Support, Totem
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (31—70), (52—111) Dex
Supports skills that summon ballista totems. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Ballista Totems from Supported Skills only Attack when you fire an Attack Projectile
Supported Skills have (40—68)% increased Totem Placement speed
Supported Skills have (25—35)% more Attack Speed
Supported Skills deal (0—9)% more Damage

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—10)% increased Totem Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
