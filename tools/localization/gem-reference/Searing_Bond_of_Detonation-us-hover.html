Searing Bond of Detonation
Spell, Totem, Fire, AoE, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (18—51) Mana
Cast Time: 1.00 sec
Requires Level (12—70), (21—98) Str, (14—68) Int
Summons a totem that casts a beam of fire at you and each other totem you control, dealing burning damage to enemies caught in the beam. Enemies near either end of a beam also suffer burning damage. Reaching maximum active totem count will cause all totems from this skill to detonate, applying the same burning damage for a duration.
+1 to maximum number of Summoned Totems per maximum Power Charge
Totem lasts 8 seconds
Deals (24.6—2977.7) Base Fire Damage per second
Base duration is 4 seconds
Modifiers to Spell Damage apply to this Skill's Damage Over Time effect
Summons a Totem which uses this Skill
Base radius is (2.3—2.7) metres
Burning Damage applied by Detonation deals 30% more Damage per Totem Detonated

Additional Effects From Quality:
Burning Damage applied by Detonation deals +(0—10)% more Damage per Totem Detonated
