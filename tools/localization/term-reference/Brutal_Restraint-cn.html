    Brutal Restraint

    Mechanics

    Brutal Restraints adds +2 or +4 Dexterity to minor passives and adds modifiers to notable passives.

    The following variants of Brutal Restraint will transform any Keystones within the radius into the following new Keystones:

    Variant Mod Keystone
    Asenath Denoted service of # dekhara in the akhara of Asenath Dance with Death
    Balbala Denoted service of # dekhara in the akhara of Balbala The Traitor
    Nasima Denoted service of # dekhara in the akhara of Nasima Second Sight

    Characters are limited to 1 Historic .

    The radius of this jewel is 1500.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.16.0
  • Brutal Restraint Timeless Jewel Notable additions now grant 8% Effect of Non-Curse Auras (previously 10%).
    • 3.15.0
  • Can no longer alter passives to grant 8% increased non-Unique Flask Effect. Instead they can now alter passives to grant '25% chance to gain Alchemist's Genius on Flask Use'.
    • 3.11.0
  • Can no longer roll copies with Wind Dancer. Can now roll copies with The Traitor (keystone passive)|The Traitor. Existing Timeless Jewels will retain the old keystones until they are rerolled with a Divine Orb.
    • 3.7.2
  • Fixed a bug where the Brutal Restraint unique Timeless Jewel was not able to drop in the Domain of Timeless Conflict.
    • 3.7.0
  • Introduced to the game.
