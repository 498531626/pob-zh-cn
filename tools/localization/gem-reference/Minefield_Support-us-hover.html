Minefield Support
Support, Mine
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (38—70), (27—48) Dex, (39—70) Int
Supports skills which throw mines.
Supported Skills throw up to 4 additional Mines
Supported Skills can have up to 3 additional Remote Mines placed at a time
Supported Skills have (46—55)% less Mine Throwing Speed
(Throwing Mines takes 10% more time for each additional Mine thrown)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—5)% increased Mine Throwing Speed
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
