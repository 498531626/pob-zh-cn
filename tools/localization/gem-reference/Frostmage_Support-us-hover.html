Frostmage Support
Exceptional, Cold, Arcane, Support, Spell
Level: (1—3)
Requires Level (72—76), (114—120) Int
Supports spell skills that deal damage with hits and have no reservation. Cannot support arcane skills, Brand skills, Orb skills, Vaal skills, or skills used by totems, traps or mines. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Supported Skills gain Added Cold Damage equal to (22—26)% of Reserved Maximum Mana
Supported Skills gain Base Mana Cost equal to 5% of Reserved Maximum Mana

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Spell Damage
