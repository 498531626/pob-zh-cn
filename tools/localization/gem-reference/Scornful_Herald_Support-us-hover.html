Scornful Herald Support
Exceptional, Critical, Support, Herald
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (72—76), (50—52) Str, (72—75) Int
Supports Herald skills causing them to have higher critical strike chance but no buff effect.
Supported Skills have +(8—12)% to Critical Strike Chance
Supported Herald Skills have no Buff Effect

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have +(0—10)% to Critical Strike Multiplier
Her bitterness blinded her to the true price of immortality.
Let Wraeclast burn and be devoured, she told herself...
and only herself, for centuries without end.
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
