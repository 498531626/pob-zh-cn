Fleshrend
Level: (1—40)
Cast Time: Instant
Projectile Speed: 8 metres per Second
Fires a projectile that turns towards enemies in front of it. The projectile does not hit enemies, but as it travels it applies a short physical damage over time debuff to each enemy in an area around it, stacking up to 3 times. The debuff also increases the damage over time they take from all sources.
