    Reflection

    Reflection is a skill granted by the unique sword The Saviour. It is similar to Vaal Double Strike .

    Mechanics

    Reflection is triggered when you use an Attack and deal a critical strike with The Saviour.

    Dual wielding The Saviour does not increase the maximum number of Mirage Saviours and will instead grant you one from each sword.

    The duration of Mirage Saviours can be modified by support gems socketed in The Saviour as well as other skill effect duration modifiers from passives or items. As such, support gems that affect Reflection are:

    • Increased Duration Support

    • Less Duration Support

    • Swift Affliction Support

    Efficacy Support Mirage Saviours use your skill, just like totems, traps, mines, doubles, or Mirage Archers do. Because that skill is yours, not their own, anything looking for stats of the skill will get your skill's stats. They are not using all your equipment. As such they are affected by Flasks like

    Lion's Roar or auras. This also means a Mirage Saviour will update its stats dynamically, for example if the player uses a flask while a Mirage Saviour is active, it will gain the flasks benefits even if it wasn't active at the time of summoning.

    However, while they are affected by your auras if these directly influence the stats of the used attack, they do not carry auras themselves. So for example Pride will only radiate from you, not from your Mirage Saviours.
