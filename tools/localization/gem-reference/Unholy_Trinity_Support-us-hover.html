Unholy Trinity Support
Exceptional, Physical, Chaos, Lightning, Support
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (72—76), (72—75) Dex, (50—52) Int
Supports skills that hit enemies, causing them to gain bonuses while you have unholy resonance of lightning, physical and chaos, and to grant unholy resonance of damage types other than the highest one in a hit. There is a maximum of 50 unholy resonance for each damage type. Cannot support triggered skills, instant skills, orb skills, brands, skills with a reservation, or skills used by totems, traps, or mines. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Hits with Supported Skills for which the highest Damage Type is Lightning, Physical or Chaos cause you to gain 25 Unholy Resonance of the other types per second for 2 seconds
Lose 5 Unholy Resonance per second of each type you are not gaining
Supported Skills deal (6—7)% more Damage per 10 of lowest Unholy Resonance
Supported Skills have (50—90)% chance to ignore Enemy Lightning and Chaos Resistance while each Unholy Resonance is at least 25
Supported Skills have (50—90)% chance to ignore Enemy Physical Damage Reduction while each Unholy Resonance is at least 25
(Maximum 50 Unholy Resonance of each type. Unholy Resonance types are Lightning, Physical and Chaos)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Damage
