Raise Zombie of Falling
Spell, Minion
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (6—24) Mana
Cast Time: 0.75 sec
Requires Level (1—70), (0—155) Int
Raises necromantic energies into the sky above a targeted location, where they coalesce into a zombie minion. The minion then attacks by falling to the ground, causing an impact that deals damage in an area. The zombie cannot survive the impact.
+200 to maximum number of Raised Zombies
Does not require a corpse

Additional Effects From Quality:
Minions have (0—15)% chance to deal Double Damage
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
