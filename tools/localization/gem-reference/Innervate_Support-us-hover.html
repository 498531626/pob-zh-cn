Innervate Support
Lightning, Support, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 120%
Requires Level (31—70), (52—111) Int
Supports any skill you use to hit enemies yourself. Cannot support skills used by totems, traps, or mines.
Gain Innervation on Killing a Shocked Enemy with Supported Skills
Innervation grants (1—8) to (17—145) Lightning Damage
Innervation lasts 8 seconds
Supported Skills have 20% chance to Shock
Supported Skills have (2—14) to (29—251) added Lightning Damage
(Shock increases Damage taken by up to 50%, depending on the amount of Lightning Damage in the hit, for 2 seconds)
