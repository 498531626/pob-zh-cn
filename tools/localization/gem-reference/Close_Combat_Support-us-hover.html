Close Combat Support
Support, Melee, Attack, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (18—70), (14—48) Str, (21—70) Dex
Supports melee attack skills. Cannot support skills which create minions.
Supported Skills deal up to (25—39)% more Melee Damage to Enemies, based on proximity
Supported Skills grant Combat Rush on Hit
Combat Rush lasts 2 seconds or until you use a Travel Skill
Combat Rush grants 20% more Attack Speed to Travel Skills not Supported by Close Combat
Supported Skills can only be used with Axes or Swords

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—10)% increased Damage with Hits to Close Range Enemies
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
