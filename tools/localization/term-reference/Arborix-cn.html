    Arborix

    Arborix is a unique Assassin Bow.

    The weapon resembles the weapon used by The Constrictor.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.17.0
  • Arborix now adds 80-100 to 200-225 Physical Damage (previously 95-115 to 240-265).
  • Arborix's basetype Assassin Bow now has 43-130 Physical Damage (previously 30-89).
    • 3.11.0
  • No longer causes you to gain Iron Reflexes for 8 seconds every 16 seconds.
  • No longer causes you to deal 30% more damage with Arrow Hits at Close Range while you have Iron Reflexes, or grants 30% increased Attack, Cast and Movement Speed or Far Shot while you don't.
  • No longer grants increased Evasion Rating or causes bow attacks to fire 2 additional arrows.
  • Now grants Iron Reflexes constantly, as well as Level 30 Dash.
  • If you have cast Dash recently, it grants 100-160% increased Evasion Rating and 20-30% increased Movement Speed.
  • If you haven't cast Dash recently, it instead grants 20-30% increased Attack Speed and causes Bow Attacks to fire 2 additional arrows.
  • Travel skills other than Dash are disabled while this is equipped.
    • 3.9.0 Arborix's basetype now has 6.7% base critical strike chance (From 6.5%). Now has an implicit that grants +15 to 25% to Critical Strike Multiplier
    3.5.0
  • Now has (95-115) to (240-265) (up from (60-70) to (180-210)) added Physical Damage.
  • Existing versions can be updated to these new values with a Divine Orb.
    • 3.1.2
  • Added effects that apply to your character while wielding Arborix, Augyre or Vulconus.
    • 3.1.0
  • Introduced to the game.
