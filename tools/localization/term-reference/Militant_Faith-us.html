    Militant Faith

    Mechanics

    Militant Faith adds +5 Devotion to minor passives and transforms a small attribute node into a +10 Devotion node. Notable passives either get added +5 Devotion or changes it. Most new notables require at least 150 Devotion for its effect to activate.

    The following variants of Militant Faith will transform any Keystones within the radius into the following new Keystones:

    Variant Mod Keystone
    Avarius Carved to glorify # new faithful converted by High Templar Avarius Power of Purpose
    Dominus Carved to glorify # new faithful converted by High Templar Dominus Inner Conviction
    Maxarius Carved to glorify # new faithful converted by High Templar Maxarius Transcendence

    Characters are limited to 1 Historic .

    The radius of this jewel is 1500.

  • Can no longer roll copies with The Agnostic. Can now roll copies with Transcendence (keystone passive)|Transcendence. Existing Timeless Jewels will retain the old keystones until they are rerolled with a Divine Orb.
