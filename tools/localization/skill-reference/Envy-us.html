    Envy

    Envy is a skill that grants an aura that adds chaos damage to the Attacks and Spells of the caster and their allies.

    Skill Functions and Interactions

    Empower Support, Enlighten Support and Enhance Support does not affect Envy since it is a skill, but not a skill gem.

    Drop restrictions

    Envy can only be obtained from United in Dream, Aul's Uprising, or Elder mod of the Elder, which can only be generated on Elder-influenced Amulets with an item level of at least 85.
