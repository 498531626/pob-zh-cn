Combustion Support
Fire, Support
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (8—70), (0—48) Str, (0—70) Int
Supports any skill that hits enemies.
Supported Skills deal (10—19)% more Fire Damage
Supported Skills have 25% chance to Ignite
Enemies Ignited by Supported Skills have -10% to Fire Resistance
(Ignite deals Fire Damage over time, based on the base Fire Damage of the Skill, for 4 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Fire Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
