Blasphemy Support
Support, Hex, Aura
Level: (1—20)
Reservation Override: 35% Mana
Cooldown Time: 1.20 sec
Requires Level (31—70), (52—111) Int
Supports hex curse skills, turning them into auras that will apply their effect to all enemies in an area around you.
Supported Curse Skills apply their Curses as Auras
Supported Curse Skills also count as Aura Skills
Using Supported Skills is Instant
25% less Effect of Supported Curses
(0—76)% increased Area of Effect of Supported Curse Skills

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—20)% increased Area of Effect of Supported Curse Skills
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
