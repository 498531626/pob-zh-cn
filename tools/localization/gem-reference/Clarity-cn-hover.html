    Clarity

    Clarity is an aura that grants mana regeneration to the caster and their allies.

    Skill Functions and Interactions

    See the aura page for more details.

    At level 20, Clarity's mana regeneration is equivalent to the natural mana regeneration of 1886 mana.

    When the player has a large amount of life but little mana and needs both the regeneration and other mana-reserving skills, linking this with Arrogance Support might be a consideration. Beware the high reservation multiplier, however.

    As Clarity reserves a flat amount of mana (whereas other Auras reserve a percentage), it can often be used at a low level without overly compromising the mana available for active skills.
