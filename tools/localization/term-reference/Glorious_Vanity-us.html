    Glorious Vanity

    Mechanics

    Glorious Vanity adds mods or replaces all minor and notable nodes in radius randomly, making it the most unpredictable of the Timeless Jewels.

    The following variants of Glorious Vanity will transform any Keystones within the radius into the following new Keystones:

    Variant Mod Keystone
    Ahuana Bathed in the blood of # sacrificed in the name of Ahuana Immortal Ambition
    Doryani Bathed in the blood of # sacrificed in the name of Doryani Corrupted Soul
    Xibaqua Bathed in the blood of # sacrificed in the name of Xibaqua Divine Flesh

    Characters are limited to 1 Historic .

    The radius of this jewel is 1500.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.16.0
  • Notable transformations now grant 7-10% Effect of Non-Curse Auras (previously 10-15%).
  • Small node transformations now grant 2-4% Effect of Non-Curse Auras (previously 3-5%).
    • 3.11.0
  • Can no longer roll copies with Eternal Youth. Can now roll copies with Immortal Ambition. Existing Timeless Jewels will retain the old keystones until they are rerolled with a Divine Orb.
  • Fixed a crash related to the Glorious Vanity keystone.
    • 3.7.0
  • Introduced to the game.
