Vampiric Link
Spell, Duration, Link
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (15—32.67) Mana per second
Cast Time: 0.50 sec
Requires Level (34—70), (35—68) Str, (50—98) Dex
Targets an allied player to apply a buff which links you to them for a duration. While linked, they get recovery from your life leech instead of you. If the target dies while linked, you will also die. This skill cannot be triggered, or used by Totems, Traps, or Mines.
Base duration is (8—9.9) seconds
Life Leech effects are not removed at Full Life
Life Leech effects apply Recovery Linked Target instead of you
Linked target's Maximum total Life Recovery per second from Leech is equal to yours
Buff grants +(2—5.8)% to Maximum total Life Recovery per second from Leech
Link breaks if target leaves range or line of sight for 4 seconds
Maximum 1 Link from any source per target
You die if Linked target dies

Additional Effects From Quality:
+(0—1.5) seconds to Base duration
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
