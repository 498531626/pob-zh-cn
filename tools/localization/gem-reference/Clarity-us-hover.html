    Vaal Clarity

    • Problem: Vaal Clarity's relatively low soul cost allows you to consistently bypass the mana costs of skills with high costs but long durations with proper timing while clearing areas. It is currently too easy to achieve this.
  • Solution: Increase the soul cost of Vaal Clarity and slightly increase its duration.
    • Vaal Clarity now has a cost 50 Souls per use (previously 30), and has a duration of 10 seconds at all gem levels (previously 8 seconds at gem Level 1, up to 9.90 at gem Level 20).
    3.13.0

    Clarity
    • Anomalous Clarity now causes you and nearby allies to have 0.25% increased Mana Recovery from Flasks per 1% gem quality (from 0.5% per 1% gem quality).
  • Divergent Clarity now grants you and nearby allies 0.25% increased damage per 1% gem quality (from 1% per 1% gem quality).
    • 3.12.0
    Vitality now grants flat life regeneration based on the level of the gem, and reserves a flat amount of mana (similar to Clarity and Precision). It can be used to more rapidly enable builds which rely on life regeneration to function (such as Righteous Fire) at lower levels, and no longer disproportionately rewards high-life builds.

        Clarity

        Clarity is an aura that grants mana regeneration to the caster and their allies.

        Skill Functions and Interactions

        See the aura page for more details.

        At level 20, Clarity's mana regeneration is equivalent to the natural mana regeneration of 1886 mana.

        When the player has a large amount of life but little mana and needs both the regeneration and other mana-reserving skills, linking this with Arrogance Support might be a consideration. Beware the high reservation multiplier, however.

        As Clarity reserves a flat amount of mana (whereas other Auras reserve a percentage), it can often be used at a low level without overly compromising the mana available for active skills.
