    Intimidating Cry

    Intimidating Cry is a warcry skill that taunts and intimidates nearby enemies and grants a buff that causes your attacks to overwhelm a portion of the enemy's physical defense, based on the power of nearby enemies. It also exerts your next attacks, making it deal double damage.
