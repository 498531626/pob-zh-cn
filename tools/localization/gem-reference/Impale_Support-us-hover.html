Impale Support
Attack, Physical, Support
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (31—70), (23—48) Str, (33—70) Dex
Supports attack skills.
Supported Attacks have 60% chance to Impale Enemies on Hit
Supported Attacks have (0—28)% increased Impale Effect
(When an Impaled enemy is hit, the Impale reflects 10% of the physical damage of the Impaling hit to that enemy. Impale lasts for 5 hits or 8 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:

This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
