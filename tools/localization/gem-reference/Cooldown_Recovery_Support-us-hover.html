Cooldown Recovery Support
Exceptional, Support
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 150%
Requires Level (72—76), (50—52) Dex, (72—75) Int
Supports skills that have a cooldown. Cannot modify the skills of minions.

Additional Effects From Quality:
