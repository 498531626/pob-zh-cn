Arcane Cloak
Spell, Duration, Guard, Lightning, Arcane
Level: (1—20)
Cooldown Time: 4.00 sec
Cast Time: Instant
Requires Level (16—70), (41—155) Int
Spends a portion of your mana to grant a buff that takes some of the damage from hits for you until depleted. The buff grants added lightning damage based on the amount of mana spent by this skill. Shares a cooldown with other Guard skills.
Base duration is 3 seconds
Spends (45—64)% of current Mana
75% of Damage from Hits is taken from Buff before your Life or Energy Shield
Buff can take Damage equal to Mana Spent by this Skill's effect
Buff grants Added Lightning Damage equal to 10% of Mana Spent by this Skill's effect
This Skill's Cooldown does not recover during its effect

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—20)% increased Buff Effect
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
