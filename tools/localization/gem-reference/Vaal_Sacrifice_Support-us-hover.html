Vaal Sacrifice Support
Exceptional, Spell, Support, Vaal
Level: (1—3)
Requires Level (72—76), (50—52) Str, (72—75) Int
Supports Vaal spell skills, making them repeat when cast at the cost of your life and energy shield. Cannot support totem skills, channelling skills, triggered skills, instant skills, retaliation skills, blink skills, or skills with a reservation.
