Sigil of Power
Spell, AoE, Duration, Lightning, Arcane
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (30—54) Mana
Cooldown Time: 10.00 sec
Cast Time: 0.50 sec
Requires Level (34—70), (79—155) Int
Places a Sigil on the ground, which grants a buff to you and allies in the area around it for a duration. The Sigil gains stages as you spend mana in its area, making the buff more powerful. You can only have one Sigil of Power at a time.
Base duration is 12 seconds
Gains a Stage when you Spend a total of (72—400) Mana while in Area
Enemies in Area deal (10—19)% less Damage while at maximum Stages
Maximum 4 Stages
Buff grants (2—7) to (29—141) Added Lightning Damage per Stage
Minimum 1 second between Stages

Additional Effects From Quality:
Enemies in Area deal +(0—4)% less damage while at maximum Stages
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
