Grants a buff which causes you to deal more damage for each Herald skill affecting you. While you have this buff, this skill turns the space around you into a tear in reality, releasing pulses that damage enemies around you. Inflicting Ailments causes you to gain Otherworldly Pressure, which increases the frequency of the pulses. You will lose Otherworldly Pressure over time, at a rate which increases the more of it you have. The damage inflicted by this skill is not affected by modifiers to spell damage.
