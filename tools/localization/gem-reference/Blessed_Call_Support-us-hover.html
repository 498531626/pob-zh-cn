Blessed Call Support
Support, Warcry, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 115%
Requires Level (31—70), (33—70) Str, (23—48) Int
Supports Warcries that are not triggered. Supported Warcries create Consecrated Ground, but have lowered area of effect and cooldown recovery.
Supported Warcries create Consecrated Ground lasting (4.1—6) seconds
Supported Warcries have (40—49)% less Area of Effect
Supported Warcries have 20% reduced Cooldown Recovery Rate
(Allies on your Consecrated Ground Regenerate a percentage of their Maximum Life per second, and Curses have 50% reduced effect on them)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—10)% increased Warcry Speed
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
