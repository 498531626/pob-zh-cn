    Elegant Hubris

    Mechanics

    Elegant Hubris removes all mods from minor passives and transforms all notable passives into new ones.

    The following variants of Elegant Hubris will transform any Keystones within the radius into the following new Keystones:

    Variant Mod Keystone
    Cadiro Commissioned # coins to commemorate Cadiro Supreme Decadence
    Caspiro Commissioned # coins to commemorate Caspiro Supreme Ostentation
    Victario Commissioned # coins to commemorate Victario Supreme Grandstanding

    Characters are limited to 1 Historic .

    The radius of this jewel is 1500.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.16.0
  • The Elegant Hubris Notable Passive Skill transformation that grants a chance to Avoid being Chilled and chance to Avoid being Shocked now grants 80% chance (previously 50%).
  • The Elegant Hubris Notable transformation now grants 12% Effect of Non-Curse Auras (previously 15%).
    • 3.11.0
  • Can no longer roll copies with Supreme Ego. Can now roll copies with Supreme Ostentation. Existing Timeless Jewels will retain the old keystones until they are rerolled with a Divine Orb.
    • 3.7.0
  • Introduced to the game.
