Trinity Support
Support
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (18—70), (14—48) Dex, (21—70) Int
Supports skills that hit enemies, causing them to gain bonuses while you have resonance of all three elements, and to grant resonance of elements other than the highest one in a hit. There is a maximum of 50 Resonance for each Element. Cannot support triggered skills, instant skills, orb skills, brands, skills with a reservation, or skills used by totems, traps, or mines. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Hits with Supported Skills for which the highest Damage Type is Elemental cause you to gain 25 Resonance of other Elements per second for 2 seconds
Lose 5 Resonance per second of each Element you are not gaining
Supported Skills deal 3% more Elemental Damage per 5 of lowest Resonance
Supported Skills penetrate (7—16)% Elemental Resistances while each Resonance is at least 25
(Maximum 50 Resonance of each Element)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Elemental Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
