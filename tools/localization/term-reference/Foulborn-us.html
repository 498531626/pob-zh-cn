Foulborn

Foulborn currenty

The Genesis Tree can grow misshapen currency orbs that work only on your Grafts, giving them new interesting mods. Sometimes, growing currency can be more successful, producing Foulborn currencies which can result in some extremely powerful outcomes.

  • Foulborn Orb of Augmentation
  • Foulborn Exalted Orb
  • Foulborn Regal Orb

Foulborn unique

The Unique Items you are growing on the Genesis Tree can also become Foulborn, mutating one of the existing modifiers on the item into a new one, which can result in some interesting new ways to use that Unique.

Not all uniques can be Foulborn, the list of uniques that can be is still ongoing however there will be more than one hundred. Uniques that come from specific sources, such as boss uniques cannot be Foulborn.
