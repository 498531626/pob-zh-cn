Divine Sentinel Support
Exceptional, AoE, Support, Minion
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 200%
Requires Level (72—76), (72—75) Str, (50—52) Int
Supports skills that create sentinel minions, causing those minions to be created with a beneficial templar aura.
Minions from Supported Skills have (30—46)% increased Area of Effect
Sentinels Summoned from Supported Skills have a Templar Aura
(Templar Auras have one of the following effects:
- Gain 15% of Physical Damage as Extra Fire Damage
- Gain 15% of Physical Damage as Extra Lightning Damage
- Regenerate 1.5% of Life per second
- 30% increased Mana Regeneration Rate
- +23% to Chaos Resistance
- 50% reduced Effect of Curses on you)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—10)% increased Minion maximum Life
"The world will never know our sacrifice, but
we will stand and fight for as long as it takes!"
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
