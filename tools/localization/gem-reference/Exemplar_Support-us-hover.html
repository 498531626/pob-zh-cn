Exemplar Support
Critical, Support, Minion
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 120%
Requires Level (18—70), (14—48) Str, (21—70) Int
Supports skills that create minions.
Minions from Supported Skills have (20—40)% increased Critical Strike Chance for each time you've Critically Struck recently with an Attack, up to 200%
Minions from Supported Skills have +(8—20)% to Critical Strike Multiplier for each time you've Critically Struck recently with an Attack, up to +100%
(Recently refers to the past 4 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Minions from Supported Skills have (0—20)% increased Critical Strike Chance
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
