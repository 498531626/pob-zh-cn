    Aspect of the Cat

    Aspect of the Cat is an aspect skill that grants two alternating buffs, each with their own duration. Cat's Stealth increases the character's stealth, gives a chance to avoid all damage from hits, and increases critical strike chance. Cat's Agility increases attack and cast speed. By default, their durations are 4 and 6 seconds respectively. The game will track the time remaining for each buff and resume counting upon reactivation (for example, you cannot rapidly toggle the skill, remove/re-equip the gem, or cycle equipment to spend more time in one buff than allotted).

    Aspect of the Cat is found on Farrul's Bite or beastcrafted onto gear as a suffix (of Farrul), by using the captured Farrul, First of the Plains unique beast. A Familiar Call divination card also rewards a Jewellery with that mod. The skill can be further modified by equipping Farrul's Chase, Farrul's Fur, (or Replica Farrul's Fur) and/or Farrul's Pounce.

    Support gems

    If crafting the Aspect of the Cat on gear that has sockets, or the Aspect skill from Farrul's Bite, the skill can be supported by socketed support gems. For example, Increased Duration Support or Less Duration Support or Efficacy Support to alter the buff duration of Cat's Stealth and Cat's Agility. Or supported by Arrogance Support to reserve life instead of mana.
