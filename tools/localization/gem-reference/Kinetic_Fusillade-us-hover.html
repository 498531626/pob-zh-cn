Kinetic Fusillade
Attack, Projectile, Duration, AoE
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (6—10) Mana
Attack Speed: 125% of base
Attack Damage: (93—107)% of base
Effectiveness of Added Damage: (93—107)%
Projectile Speed: 20 metres per Second
Requires Level (12—70), (33—155) Int
Requires Wands
Wave your wand to create a hovering kinetic projectile. After a short duration, the projectile is fired at an enemy, dealing area damage on impact and chaining to further targets. Using this attack again will reset the duration for all hovering projectiles. Multiple projectiles fired in sequence will attempt to target different enemies. Cannot be directly used by traps or mines.
150% Arcane Might
Base duration is 0.5 seconds
Chains +3 Times
Base radius is 1.2 metres
Projectiles deal (3—8)% more Damage per previous Projectile fired in sequence
Maximum 12 hovering Projectiles
(Arcane Might causes Increases and Reductions to Spell Damage to also apply to Attack Damage at that percentage of their value)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Projectiles deal +(0—4)% more Damage per previous Projectile fired in sequence
