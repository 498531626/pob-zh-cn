Wintertide Brand
Spell, AoE, Cold, Duration, Brand
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (6—16) Mana
Cast Time: 0.70 sec
Requires Level (12—70), (33—155) Int
Creates a magical brand which can attach to a nearby enemy, dealing cold damage over time and chilling them. It periodically activates while attached, gaining stages that raise the damage. When removed, a short-duration debuff dealing the same damage over time and chill is applied to each nearby enemy. The brand keeps its charges while detached.
Activates every 0.25 seconds while Attached
Increases and Reductions to Cast Speed also apply to this Skill's Activation frequency
Deals (13.5—389.8) Base Cold Damage per second
Can have 1 additional Brands Attached to an Enemy
Modifiers to Spell Damage apply to this Skill's Damage Over Time effect
Debuff deals 20% more Damage per Stage
Maximum 20 Stages
Can be Detached for a total Duration of 6 seconds
Can be Attached for a total Duration of 2 seconds
Base Debuff Duration is 2 seconds

Additional Effects From Quality:
Debuff deals +(0—5)% more Damage per Stage
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
