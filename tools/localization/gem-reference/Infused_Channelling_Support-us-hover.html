Infused Channelling Support
Support, Channelling
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (4—70), (0—48) Dex, (0—70) Int
Supports any channelling skill, protecting you from damage while you channel it. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Supported Skills deal (20—29)% more Damage
While Channelling a Supported Skill, take 12% less Damage from Hits

Additional Effects From Quality:
While Channelling a Supported Skill, take (0—2)% less Damage from Hits
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
