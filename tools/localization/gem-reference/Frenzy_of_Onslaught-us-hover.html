Frenzy of Onslaught
Attack, Melee, Strike
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (7—10) Mana
Attack Damage: (163—353)% of base
Effectiveness of Added Damage: (163—353)%
Requires Level (16—70), (41—155) Dex
Requires One Hand Swords, Thrusting One Hand Swords, Two Hand Swords, One Hand Axes, Two Hand Axes, One Hand Maces, Sceptres, Two Hand Maces, Staves, Unarmed, Warstaves, Daggers, Rune Daggers, Claws
Perform a strike with a melee weapon that gives the character a frenzy charge if it hits. Frenzy charges increase your attack speed.
Consume all Frenzy Charges when you Hit an enemy while at maximum Frenzy Charges to gain Onslaught for 1 second per Charge consumed
5% more Attack Damage per Frenzy Charge
5% more Attack Speed per Frenzy Charge
(Onslaught grants 20% increased Attack, Cast, and Movement Speed)

Additional Effects From Quality:
+(0—2)% more Attack Damage per Frenzy Charge
+(0—2)% more Attack Speed per Frenzy Charge
