Wall of Force
Spell, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (9—33) Mana
Cooldown Time: 9.00 sec
Cast Time: 0.50 sec
Requires Level (16—70), (41—155) Int
Creates a wall of force which allies and enemies can pass through, but allied projectiles which can collide with enemies will also collide with this wall, and can chain from it to other targets. Projectiles can only collide with a single Wall of Force, and no more than once.
Base duration is (5.1—7) seconds
Wall will be (4.5—6) meters long
Projectiles cannot Split, Pierce, or Fork when colliding with the wall
Projectiles can Chain an additional time when colliding with the wall
Projectiles can only chain to targets on the same side of the wall
Projectiles which have collided with the wall deal (20—30)% less Damage
Wall is destroyed after (11—17) Projectile collisions

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—20)% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
