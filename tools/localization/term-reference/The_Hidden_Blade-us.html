    The Hidden Blade

    The Hidden Bladeis a unique Ambusher.

    Mechanics

    • The trigger skill, Unseen Strike,shares a cooldown with all versions of itself. If you have 2 'The Hidden Blade' equipped, only 1 of them will activate at a time, typically the one held in the Main hand (See Trigger Order).

    • Supports gems in The Hidden Blade affect Unseen Strike. (See the skill for more info)
