Eye of Winter
Spell, Projectile, Cold
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (14—26) Mana
Cast Time: 0.80 sec
Critical Strike Chance: 7.50%
Effectiveness of Added Damage: 55%
Projectile Speed: 3 metres per Second
Requires Level (28—70), (67—155) Int
Fires a single eye projectile which constantly releases shard projectiles in a spiral. The shards will damage enemies they impact, dealing cold damage. The eye cannot damage enemies. When the eye dissipates or collides with terrain, it releases a faster spiral of extra shards.
Deals (16—245) to (25—367) Cold Damage
Modifiers to number of Projectiles only apply to the final Spiral
Fires 8 Shard Projectiles in a Spiral when Projectile ends
Projectiles continuously gain Damage, up to 200% more Damage by the time they dissipate

Additional Effects From Quality:
Fires +(0—2) Shard Projectiles in a Spiral when Projectile ends
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
