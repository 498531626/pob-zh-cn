Bloodlust Support
Attack, Physical, Support, Melee
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (18—70), (33—111) Str
Supports melee attack skills, causing them to deal more damage against bleeding enemies, but preventing those skills from inflicting bleeding on enemies in any way.
Supported Skills deal (25—39)% more Melee Physical Damage against Bleeding enemies
Supported Attacks cannot cause Bleeding

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Melee Damage against Bleeding Enemies
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
