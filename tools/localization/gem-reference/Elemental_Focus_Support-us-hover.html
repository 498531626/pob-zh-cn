Elemental Focus Support
Support
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (18—70), (33—111) Int
Supports any skill that hits or burns enemies, or inflicts elemental ailments.
Supported Skills deal (20—34)% more Elemental Damage
Supported Skills cannot inflict Elemental Ailments
(Elemental Ailments are Ignited, Scorched, Chilled, Frozen, Brittle, Shocked, and Sapped)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Elemental Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
