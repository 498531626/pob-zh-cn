Splitting Steel of Ammunition
Attack, Projectile, AoE, Physical
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (6—10) Mana
Attack Damage: (120—387)% of base
Effectiveness of Added Damage: (120—387)%
Requires Level (1—70), (0—68) Str, (0—98) Dex
Requires One Hand Swords, Thrusting One Hand Swords, Two Hand Swords, One Hand Axes, Two Hand Axes
Consume Steel Shards to fire a single projectile that splits on impact or at the targeted location, dealing area damage when it splits and again when the split projectiles explode at the end of their flight. Projectiles from this skill cannot return. Requires a Sword or Axe. Steel Shards are gained with the Call of Steel Skill.
Modifiers to number of Projectiles instead apply to
the number of targets Projectile Splits towards
Projectiles Split towards (4—7) targets
Base radius is (1.4—1.8) metres
If you have no Steel Shards, Projectile counts as already having Split
60% chance to Impale Enemies on Hit
Projectiles can Split without hitting a target
Consumes up to 2 Steel Shards
(When an Impaled enemy is hit, the Impale reflects 10% of the physical damage of the Impaling hit to that enemy. Impale lasts for 5 hits or 8 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—40)% increased Impale Effect
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
