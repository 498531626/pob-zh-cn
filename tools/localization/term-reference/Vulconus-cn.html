    Vulconus

    Modifiers

    The Avatar of Fire mod has the same effect as the Avatar of Fire keystone.

    Animated Weapons or Animated Guardians wielding this weapon will gain the Avatar of Fire Keystone.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.14.0
  • (Undocumented) Several Simulacrum, Legion, Shaper Guardian, Elder Guardian, and Conqueror Drop-Restricted Uniques have been changed to be core Uniques instead. These no longer drop deterministically from their original sources.
    • 3.8.0
  • Several Daggers are now classified as Rune Daggers. Daggers can no longer roll caster-related modifiers, while Rune Daggers retain their old mod generation behaviour.
    • 3.5.0
  • No longer has added fire damage against bleeding enemies, added physical damage against ignited enemies or chance to inflict bleeding against ignited enemies.
  • It now has (130-160) to (220-240) added fire damage and (85-110) to (130-150) added physical damage.
  • Now grants (70-100)% increased Fire damage versus bleeding enemies and (70-100)% increased physical damage versus ignited enemies.
  • Now has 50% chance to inflict bleeding.
  • It now grants 160-200% (up from 80-120%) increased chance to critically strike while you have Avatar of Fire and 2000 (up from 1000) armour while you do not have Avatar of Fire.
  • Existing versions can be updated to these new values with a Divine Orb.
  • Items exclusive to the Shaper's guardians and the Elder's guardians now drop less frequently. Uniques exclusive to the Elder's guardians now also drop based on map tier, with some items unable to drop until higher map tiers.
  • (undocumented) Can drop from any Elder guardian
    • 3.1.2
  • Added effects that apply to your character while wielding Vulconus.
    • 3.1.0
  • Introduced to the game.
