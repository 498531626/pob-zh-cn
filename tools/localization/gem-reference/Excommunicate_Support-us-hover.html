Excommunicate Support
Lightning, Fire, Physical, Support, Melee, Attack, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (31—70), (33—70) Str, (23—48) Int
Supports melee attacks. Does not modify skills used by minions.
Supported Skills gain (7—16)% of Physical Damage as Extra Fire Damage
Supported Skills gain (8—17)% of Physical Damage as Extra Lightning Damage
Supported Skills Excommunicate Enemies on Melee Hit for 3 seconds
(Excommunicated Enemies cannot deal Chaos Damage)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Physical Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
