Pact of Beidat
Exceptional, Projectile, Chaining, AoE, Spell, Pact
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (16—33) Mana
Cooldown Time: 4.00 sec
Cast Time: 0.80 sec
Requires Level (24—70)
Cast a pact, Empowering non-channelling spells that either fire projectiles, apply an effect to an area around a targeted location or fire chaining beams, causing them to deal more damage and have enhanced coverage. However, using this skill also burdens you with an Affliction.
Empowered Skills deal (16—35)% more Damage with Hits and Ailments
Empowers the next 6 Spells you Cast
Empowered Skills also affect (5—8) additional areas in a spiral around the targeted area
Beams from Empowered Skills Chain (4—6) additional times
Empowered Skills fire (4—6) additional Projectiles
Empowered Skills fire Projectiles in a circle
(Ailments that deal Damage are Bleeding, Ignited, and Poisoned)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Empowers the next +(0—2) Spells you Cast
Wield the gifts of Beidat, luminary of the pale unseeing,
whose honeyed words bore tunnels through pliant minds.
