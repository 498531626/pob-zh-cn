Spell Totem Support
Support, Spell, Totem
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 200%
Requires Level (8—70), (0—70) Str, (0—48) Int
Supports spell skills that are not triggered. Instead of casting that spell, you will summon a totem that casts the spell for you.
Supported Skills deal (50—55)% less Damage
Supported Skills will summon a Totem which uses that Skill
Totem lasts 8 seconds
Supported Skills have 40% less Cast Speed

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—10)% increased Totem Placement speed
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
