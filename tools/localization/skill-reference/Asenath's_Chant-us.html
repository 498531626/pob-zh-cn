    Asenath's Chant

    Mechanics

    Trigger a Socketed Spell when you Attack with a Bow: Each time you attack with a bow, a socketed spell will be cast. Each spell has a separate 0.3 second cooldown. The triggered spells are targeted where the attack was targeted.

    Note that the game usually prevent a skill to link with two trigger mechanics. For example, you CANNOT socket a spell in Asenath's Chant which is support by Hextouch Support and another curse skill (which curse skills are spells). That would make the curse is supported by both Hextouch and the mod Trigger a Socketed Spell when you Attack with a Bow, and making the curse unable to be triggered.
