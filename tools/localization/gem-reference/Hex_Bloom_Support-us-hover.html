Hex Bloom Support
Support, Hex
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (38—70), (63—111) Int
Supports hex curse skills. Hexes from auras or cursed ground cannot transfer.
Supported Skills have (0—28)% increased Hex Transfer range
Hexes from Supported Skills Transfer to all Enemies within 3 metres when Hexed Enemy dies
(Hexes from Auras or Cursed Ground cannot be Transferred)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—4)% increased Hex Transfer range
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
