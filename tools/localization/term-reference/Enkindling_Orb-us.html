Enkindling Orb

Enkindling Orb provides a boost to your utility flasks in exchange for preventing charge gain while they’re active. It is a new currency item in Path of Exile: Expedition.

  • Adds an enchantment to a utility flask that will improve it but prevent it from gaining charges during its effect. Replaces any existing enchantment.

It prevents Utility Flasks from gaining charges while active but provides a big boost to their effect, duration, or various ways they interact with their charges. There are various different types of boosts, so you can use more Orbs to reroll for the one you want.

This item can be found as a drop throughout the game.

Vendor recipes

They can be obtained by the following vendor recipe:
