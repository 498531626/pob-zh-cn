Gluttony Support
Exceptional, Support, Projectile, Spell, Duration
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (72—76), (50—52) Dex, (72—75) Int
Supports Projectile Spells you cast yourself. Cannot support orb skills, triggered skills, brand skills, Vaal skills, channelling skills, skills with a reservation, or skills used by things other than you.
Gain 1 Gluttony on casting Supported Spells
On reaching 10 Gluttony, lose all Gluttony and gain Disgorge for 4 seconds
While you have Disgorge Supported Spells fire Projectiles in a circle
While you have Disgorge Supported Spells fire (5—7) additional Projectiles
(You cannot gain Gluttony while you have Disgorge)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Projectile Damage
Champions of the Tangle are driven to seek
sustenance for their master; should they fall
behind, they would soon be consumed as well.
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
