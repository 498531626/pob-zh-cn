Siphoning Trap
Trap, Spell, Duration, AoE, Cold
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (7—24) Mana
Cooldown Time: 4.00 sec
Cast Time: 1.00 sec
Requires Level (10—70), (0—68) Dex, (18—98) Int
Throws a trap that applies debuff beams to a number of nearby enemies for a duration. The beams chill enemies and deal cold damage over time. A beam is also applied to you, granting life and mana regeneration based on the number of debuff beams affecting enemies.
Trap lasts 4 seconds
Base duration is 3 seconds
Deals (16.5—908.2) Base Cold Damage per second
Modifiers to Spell Damage apply to this Skill's Damage Over Time effect
Base radius is 3 metres
Applies beams to the closest 10 Enemies
Regenerate (12.7—324.4) Life per second while any Enemy is affected
Regenerate (2.9—17.2) Mana per second while any Enemy is affected
Regenerate (1.3—32.4) Life per second for each affected Enemy
Regenerate (0.3—1.7) Mana per second for each affected Enemy

Additional Effects From Quality:
Applies beams to the closest +(0—4) Enemies
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
