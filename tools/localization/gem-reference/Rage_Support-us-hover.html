Rage Support
Support, Melee, Attack
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 130%
Requires Level (18—70), (33—111) Str
Supports melee attack skills. Minions cannot gain rage.
Gain 3 Rage on Attack Hit
Supported Skills have (10—19)% increased Attack Speed while you have at least 10 Rage
(Every Rage grants 1% more Attack Damage. Maximum Rage is 30. Lose 10 Rage per second if you have not been Hit or gained Rage in the last 2 seconds)
(Only one Hit every 0.5 seconds can cause you to gain Rage)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Attack Damage
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
