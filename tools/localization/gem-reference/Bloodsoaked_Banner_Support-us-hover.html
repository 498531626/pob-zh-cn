Bloodsoaked Banner Support
Exceptional, Physical, Support, Duration
Level: (1—3)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 1000%
Requires Level (72—76), (72—75) Str, (50—52) Dex
Supports banner skills. Cannot modify the skills of minions.
Supported Skills Cost Life instead of Mana
Supported Banners inflict Corrupted Blood every second to enemies within their area
Corrupted Blood inflicted by Supported Banners deals (20.9—26.7) base Physical Damage per second per Valour consumed
Corrupted Blood inflicted by Supported Banners has a Base Duration of 8 seconds

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—10)% increased Area of Effect
"The history of Wraeclast is marked by bloody conflicts. The rise
and fall of empires. Man slaughtered man, never suspecting the
madness lurking under the surface might not have been innate."
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
