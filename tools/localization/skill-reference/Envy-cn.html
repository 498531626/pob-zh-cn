    忌妒

    忌妒 is a skill that grants an 光环 that adds 混沌伤害 to the 攻击s and 法术s of the caster and their allies.

    Skill Functions and Interactions

    赋予辅助, 启蒙辅助 and 增幅辅助 does not affect Envy since it is a skill, but not a skill gem.

    Drop restrictions

    Envy can only be obtained from 合流梦寐, 奥尔的崛起, or Elder mod of the Elder, which can only be generated on Elder-influenced Amulets with an item level of at least 85.
