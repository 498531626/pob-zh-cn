Cruelty Support
Support, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (18—70), (21—70) Str, (14—48) Int
Supports any skill that hits enemies. Minions cannot gain Cruelty.
Supported Skills deal (15—24)% more Damage with Hits
Cruelty has a Base Duration of 4 seconds
Hits from Supported Skills grant Cruelty
(0—19)% increased Effect of Cruelty granted by Supported Skills
(Cruelty makes all Skills Supported by Cruelty Support deal up to 40% more Damage over Time, based on the amount of Damage in the Hit which grants it)

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—5)% increased Effect of Cruelty granted by Supported Skills
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
