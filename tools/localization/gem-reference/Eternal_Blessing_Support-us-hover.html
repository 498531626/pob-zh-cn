Eternal Blessing Support
Support, Aura, Blessing
Level: (1—20)
Requires Level (38—70), (63—111) Str
Supports aura skills that reserve life or mana to create auras around you.
Supported Skills have (0—19)% increased Aura effect
Supported Skills have no Reservation
Supported Skills also count as Blessing Skills
You can only have one Aura on you from your Blessing Skills
Your Non-Blessing Skills which Reserve Mana are Disabled while you have an Aura from a Supported Skill on you

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills have (0—5)% increased Aura effect
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
