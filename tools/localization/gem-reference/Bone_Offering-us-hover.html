Bone Offering
Minion, Spell, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (16—33) Mana
Cast Time: 1.00 sec
Requires Level (12—70), (33—155) Int
Consumes a corpse, granting all of your minions the power to block both attacks and spells. The skill consumes other nearby corpses, increasing the duration for each corpse consumed.
Base duration is 5 seconds
Additional 1 seconds Base Duration per extra corpse Consumed
Grants Minions +(25—35)% Chance to Block Attack Damage
Grants Minions +(25—34)% Chance to Block Spell Damage
Grants Minions 4% of Life Regenerated per Second if they have Blocked recently
(Recently refers to the past 4 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—10)% increased Effect of Offering
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
