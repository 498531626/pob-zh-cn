Swift Affliction Support
Support, Duration
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 140%
Requires Level (31—70), (52—111) Dex
Supports any skill that has a duration, or can hit enemies to inflict ailments on them.
Supported Skills deal (25—39)% more Damage over Time
25% less Duration of Supported Skills and Damaging Ailments they inflict
(Ailments that deal Damage are Bleeding, Ignited, and Poisoned)

Additional Effects From Quality:
Supported Skills deal (0—10)% increased Damage over Time
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
