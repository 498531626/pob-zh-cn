Skills Tutorial /6
一种火系投射物类范围法术，对敌人造成火焰伤害。有较高几率点燃敌人，持续造成火焰伤害。
增加燃烧伤害(辅)
大幅提升火球的点燃效果带来的火焰伤害。
几率点燃(辅)
提高火球的火焰伤害和点燃敌人的几率，并降低被点燃敌人的火焰抗性，是理想的火系法术全能辅助。
致命异常状态(辅)
降低火球的命中伤害，换取更多异常状态伤害。因此配合注重提升点燃持续伤害的火球辅助效果尤其强大。
点燃扩散(辅)
增加火球点燃敌人的几率，同时允许点燃效果在敌人之中扩散。
多重投射(辅)
通过增加投射物数量，火球现在可同时命中的目标更多了。 达到更高级别后，利用高阶多重投射宝石还可打击更大范围内的敌人。
极速腐化(辅)
降低点燃的持续时间，但提高点燃造成的伤害，让点燃效果在更短时间内发挥更大威力。

    Fireball

    Fireball is a spell that fires a projectile, dealing fire damage with an increased chance to Ignite.

    Skill functions and interactions

    • Area of Effect: The radius of the explosion caused by collision (with either an enemy or a terrain obstruction) is affected by all AoE increases, with a base radius of 9. Full spell damage is done to any enemy in range of the explosion.

    • Pierce, Chain, Fork: Enemies hit by a projectile that pierces, chains, or forks will take full damage from the hit, but the projectile will not explode until it hits an enemy and does not pierce, chain, or fork, or hits a wall.

    • Shotgunning: Each enemy can only be hit directly by one projectile per cast. However, if an enemy is in range of the explosion effect of more than one projectile from the same cast, and those projectiles each exploded on different enemies or a wall, it will take full damage from each explosion.

    • Can be used in left mouse button slot to cast and move.
