Sniper's Mark
Spell, Curse, Mark
Level: (1—20)
Cost: (16—33) Mana
Cast Time: 0.50 sec
Requires Level (4—70), (16—155) Dex
Curses a single enemy, increasing the damage they take from projectiles, and making projectiles split when hitting them, to hit other targets around them. You can only have one Mark at a time.
Cursed enemies take (10—29)% increased Damage from Projectile Hits
Projectiles which Hit Cursed Enemies Split towards 2 additional targets

Additional Effects From Quality:
Cursed enemies take (0—5)% increased Damage from Projectile Hits
Place into an item socket to gain this skill. Right click to remove from a socket.
