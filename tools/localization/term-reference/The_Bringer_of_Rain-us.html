    The Bringer of Rain

    Modifiers

    Applicable skill gems socketed in this item are supported by level 18 Melee Physical Damage Support, level 18 Faster Attacks Support, and level 18 Blind Support. All support stats are accounted for, including the mana multiplier. This leads to a total mana multiplier of 161%.

    Version history

    VersionChanges
    3.19.0
  • Now has Socketed Gems are Supported by Level 30 Melee Physical Damage Support, Faster Attacks Support, and Blind Support (previously all level 18).
  • Now has +200-300 to maximum Life (previously +200-220).
  • The changes to the Level of Supported Gems applies to existing items, though you will need to use a Divine Orb to fix the descriptions or receive the new maximum Life values.
    • 3.5.0
  • Now support socketed skills with level 18 Blind Support and Faster Attacks Support. This change also affects very, very old versions of '''Bringer of Rain''' for which these were the original support levels.
  • A Divine Orb will NOT update existing items to these new values, however it will update the Life values on '''Bringer of Rain''' to the new values of 200-220 (up from 120-160) Life.
  • * (wiki editor note) This line refers to item dropped between version 1.1 to 3.4, which use different item mods with the legacy items that dropped before version 1.1.
    • 1.3.0
  • Chance to block has been reduced from 15% to 6%. The chance to gain an endurance charge when you block has been increased from 10% to 20%.
  • Introduced 3D art for this item's alternate artwork version.
    • 1.1.0d
  • You can now see your body armour while wearing The Bringer of Rain. This includes cosmetic microtransactions such as Wings and the Seraph Armour skin.
    • 1.1.0
  • Maximum Life has been reduced from 200-220 to 120-160. The supporting Blind is now level 6 and the supporting Faster Attacks is now level 12. On a legacy Bringer of Rain item, a Divine Orb will reroll the Maximum Life, but not the level of the supports.
    • 0.11.4
  • The Bringer of Rain now has its own 3D art.
    • 0.11.1
  • The name of the basetype has changed from Crested Helmet to Nightmare Bascinet.
    • 0.10.8c
  • Introduced to the game.
