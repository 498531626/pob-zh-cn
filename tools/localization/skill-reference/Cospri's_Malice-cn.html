    Cospri's Malice

    Mechanics

    When an attack with a melee skill made with Cospri's Malice hits an enemy and deals a critical strike, socketed cold spells will be cast, similar to a trigger gem. The cooldown of this triggering effect is 150ms.

    Multiple socketed cold spells will trigger sequentially, starting with the socket on the top left. The order of the triggered spells is preserved even if a previous spell is already off cooldown. Multiple copies of the same spell share a cooldown. NOTE! This applies on multiple copies of the same spell when dual-wielding, even if they are in different Cospri's.

    Dual wielding: While dual wielding, socketed cold spells will only cast when a melee critical strike is made with the Cospri's they are socketed in.
