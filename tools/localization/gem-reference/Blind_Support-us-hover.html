Blind Support
Support
Level: (1—20)
Cost & Reservation Multiplier: 110%
Requires Level (8—70), (18—111) Dex
Supports any skill that hits enemies.
Supported Skills have 10% chance to Blind enemies on hit
Supported Skills have (0—38)% increased Blinding duration
(Being Blinded causes 20% less Accuracy Rating and Evasion Rating, for 4 seconds)

Additional Effects From Quality:
(0—10)% increased Blind Effect
This is a Support Gem. It does not grant a bonus to your character, but to skills in sockets connected to it. Place into an item socket connected to a socket containing the Skill Gem you wish to augment. Right click to remove from a socket.
